Items we source

Examination gloves

Non-sterile gloves for routine examinations, in nitrile, latex or vinyl, usually sold in boxes of 100.

When you ask for a price, tell us: Material, powder-free or powdered, size (XS to XL) and number of boxes.

Surgical gloves

Sterile gloves packed in pairs for surgery and sterile procedures, sized by hand size.

When you ask for a price, tell us: Latex or latex-free, size (for example 6.5, 7, 7.5, 8) and number of pairs or boxes.

Face masks and respirators

Medical face masks for clinical use, and respirators such as N95, KN95 and FFP2 for airborne protection.

When you ask for a price, tell us: Mask type or standard (for example Type IIR, N95), ear-loop or tie-on, and quantity.

Gowns, drapes and caps

Disposable isolation gowns, sterile surgical gowns, surgical drapes, bouffant caps and shoe covers.

When you ask for a price, tell us: Sterile or non-sterile, size, fabric weight if you know it, and quantity.

Syringes and needles

Disposable syringes from 1 ml to 50 ml, insulin syringes and hypodermic needles in standard gauges.

When you ask for a price, tell us: Syringe volume, needle gauge and length, Luer lock or Luer slip, and quantity.

IV cannulas and giving sets

Peripheral IV cannulas in colour-coded gauges, infusion giving sets and extension lines.

When you ask for a price, tell us: Gauge (for example 18G, 20G, 22G), with or without injection port, and quantity.

Catheters and urine bags

Foley catheters, suction catheters, feeding tubes and urine drainage bags.

When you ask for a price, tell us: Type, size in French (Fr), balloon size for Foley catheters, and quantity.

Gauze, swabs and dressings

Gauze swabs, gauze rolls, cotton, crepe bandages, adhesive dressings and tapes.

When you ask for a price, tell us: Sterile or non-sterile, size, ply and quantity.

Specimen and blood collection

Urine and stool containers, sample tubes, blood collection tubes and needles, and swabs.

When you ask for a price, tell us: Container or tube type, volume, additive (for blood tubes) and quantity.

Questions

Can I order surgical disposables in small quantities?

Yes. Tell us the quantity you need, whether that is one box or a larger order for a clinic, and we price that quantity.

Do I need to know the exact standard or size?

It helps, but it is not required. Describe what you use now, or send a photo of the box, and we will match the item.

Are surgical disposables regulated in Pakistan?

Many are medical devices under DRAP's Medical Devices Rules 2017. Ask about a specific item and we will tell you what documentation the supplier provides. See our DRAP guide for the basics.

Do you deliver outside Islamabad?

Yes. Items are imported on order and supplied across Pakistan. Tell us your city when you request a quote.

What types of syringes are there?

  • Standard disposable syringe. With or without a needle, in volumes such as 1, 3, 5, 10, 20 and 50 ml.
  • Insulin syringe. Marked in insulin units, with a fine fixed needle.
  • Auto-disable syringe. Locks after one use to prevent reuse.
  • Luer lock syringe. Screws onto needles and lines for a secure fit.

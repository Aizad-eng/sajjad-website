Oxygen concentrator price in Pakistan

An oxygen concentrator draws in room air and delivers oxygen-enriched air through a nasal cannula or mask. It should be used as prescribed by a doctor.

What types of oxygen concentrator are there?

  • Home (stationary) concentrator. Mains powered, with a set flow range such as up to 5 or 10 litres per minute.
  • Portable concentrator. Battery powered and lighter. Many deliver oxygen in pulses rather than a continuous flow, so check it suits the prescription.

What changes the oxygen concentrator price?

  • Flow range (litres per minute)
  • Continuous or pulse delivery
  • Oxygen concentration and alarms
  • Size and weight for freight
  • Exchange rate on the day

What should you check before you buy?

  • That the flow and delivery type match the prescription
  • Oxygen purity indicator and alarms
  • 220V power and backup plan for power cuts
  • Noise level and filters

What types of pulse oximeter are there?

  • Fingertip pulse oximeter. Small, battery powered and simple. Common for home use.
  • Handheld pulse oximeter. Uses a separate probe, often with adult and paediatric probes. Common in clinics and ambulances.
  • Tabletop or bedside oximeter. Continuous monitoring with alarms, used in wards.

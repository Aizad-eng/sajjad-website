What types of bp apparatus are there?

  • Digital upper-arm BP monitor. Automatic monitor with an upper-arm cuff. Generally the recommended choice for home monitoring.
  • Digital wrist BP monitor. Smaller and portable, but more sensitive to wrist position.
  • Aneroid BP apparatus. Manual dial gauge with cuff and bulb, used with a stethoscope by trained staff. Needs periodic checking against a reference.

What types of surgical gloves are there?

  • Sterile surgical gloves. Packed in pairs, sized by hand size, for surgery and sterile procedures.
  • Nitrile examination gloves. Latex-free and puncture resistant, sold in boxes.
  • Latex examination gloves. Good fit and feel, not for people with latex allergy.
  • Vinyl examination gloves. Lower cost, for short low-risk tasks.

Hearing aids come in behind-the-ear, receiver-in-canal and in-ear styles. A hearing test and fitting by an audiologist matter as much as the device.

What types of hearing aid are there?

  • Behind-the-ear (BTE). Sits behind the ear with a tube or earmould. Suits a wide range of hearing loss.
  • Receiver-in-canal (RIC). Small body behind the ear with the speaker in the ear canal.
  • In-the-ear (ITE) and in-the-canal (CIC). Fits in the ear or canal and is less visible.

What changes the hearing aid price?

  • Style and technology level
  • Rechargeable or battery
  • Programmable or not
  • Exchange rate on the day

What should you check before you buy?

  • A hearing test and recommendation from an audiologist first
  • Who will programme and fit the aid
  • Battery or charger availability
  • Domes, tubes and earmould spares

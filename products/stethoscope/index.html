What types of stethoscope are there?

  • Single-head stethoscope. One chestpiece. Light and simple.
  • Dual-head stethoscope. A diaphragm and a bell, for high and low frequency sounds.
  • Cardiology stethoscope. Heavier chestpiece and better acoustics for heart sounds.
  • Paediatric stethoscope. Smaller chestpiece for children.
  • Digital stethoscope. Amplifies sound and in some models records it or connects to an app.

What changes the stethoscope price?

What should you check before you buy?

Glucometer price in Pakistan

A glucometer measures blood glucose from a small drop of blood on a test strip. The strips are specific to each meter, so the meter you choose decides the strips you buy for years.

What types of glucometer are there?

  • Blood glucose meter with strips. The common type: a lancet pricks the finger and the meter reads a strip.
  • Continuous glucose monitor (CGM). A small sensor worn on the body that reads glucose continuously. Sensors are replaced on a schedule set by the maker.

What changes the glucometer price?

  • Meter model and the price of its strips
  • Number of strips and lancets in the box
  • Memory and app connection
  • Exchange rate on the day

What should you check before you buy?

  • That strips for the meter are easy to get
  • Strip expiry dates when you buy in larger quantities
  • Sample size and test time
  • Lancing device and lancets included

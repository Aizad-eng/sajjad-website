Hospital bed price in Pakistan

Hospital beds for wards and home care come as manual crank beds or electric beds. They are large items, so freight is a big part of the price.

What types of hospital bed are there?

  • Manual crank bed. Positions are changed with hand cranks, usually two or three functions.
  • Electric bed. Positions are changed with a motor and handset.
  • ICU bed. Electric bed with more positions and accessories for intensive care.

What changes the hospital bed price?

  • Manual or electric, and number of functions
  • Mattress, side rails, IV pole and castors
  • Size and weight for freight
  • Exchange rate on the day

What should you check before you buy?

  • Room size and door width for delivery
  • Weight capacity
  • Side rails and lockable castors
  • Mattress included or separate

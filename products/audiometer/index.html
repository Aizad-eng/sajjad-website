Audiometer price in Pakistan

Audiometers are used by ENT clinics, audiologists and screening programmes to test hearing. They range from portable screening units to full diagnostic systems.

What types of audiometer are there?

  • Screening audiometer. Air conduction testing for hearing screening.
  • Diagnostic audiometer. Air and bone conduction, masking and often speech testing.
  • Clinical audiometer. Full-featured systems for specialist audiology clinics.

What changes the audiometer price?

  • Screening, diagnostic or clinical
  • Transducers included (headphones, insert earphones, bone conductor)
  • PC software or stand-alone
  • Exchange rate on the day

What should you check before you buy?

  • The tests you need to run
  • Transducers and their calibration
  • Availability of replacement headphones and bone conductors
  • Power supply and warranty support

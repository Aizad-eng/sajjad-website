About SurgiSource

SurgiSource sources medical equipment and surgical disposables for clinics, hospitals, laboratories and individuals in Pakistan.

What we do

We find the item you need from manufacturers and verified suppliers, price it in rupees, import it on order and deliver it anywhere in Pakistan. We are based in Islamabad.

One example: a customer needed a replacement audiometric headset that no shop in Pakistan stocked. We found it from a verified supplier, bought it as a single piece and delivered it in Pakistan.

What we don't do

  • We don't keep stock. Items are imported on order.
  • We don't install, calibrate or service equipment. Arrange that with a local biomedical engineer.
  • We don't give medical advice. Our guides are about buying equipment, not about treatment.

Contact

Every enquiry goes through our quote form. We reply on WhatsApp.

Questions

What is a validated blood pressure monitor?

One that has passed a clinical accuracy test against a recognised protocol. Our guide explains how to check a model before you buy.

Can I order one device for home use?

Yes. We supply clinics, hospitals, labs and individuals, and you can order a single device.

Do test strips have to match the meter?

Yes. Glucometer strips are made for specific meters, so tell us your meter model when you ask for strips.

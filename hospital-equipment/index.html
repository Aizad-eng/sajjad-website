Patient monitors

Bedside monitors for parameters such as ECG, SpO2, blood pressure and temperature.

When you ask for a price, tell us: The parameters you need, screen size, and accessories such as cuffs and probes.

Suction machines

Portable and ward suction units for airway and surgical suction.

When you ask for a price, tell us: Portable or ward use, flow rate needs, and spare jars or tubing.

Nebulisers

Compressor and mesh nebulisers with adult and child masks.

When you ask for a price, tell us: Compressor or mesh, and the masks and kits you need.

Infusion and syringe pumps

Volumetric infusion pumps and syringe pumps for controlled drug delivery.

When you ask for a price, tell us: Pump type, compatible syringe sizes or sets, and quantity.

Mobility aids

Wheelchairs, walkers, crutches and commode chairs.

When you ask for a price, tell us: Type, size, folding or fixed, and weight capacity.

Oxygen accessories

Flow meters, humidifier bottles, nasal cannulas and oxygen masks.

When you ask for a price, tell us: Connector type, adult or paediatric, and quantity.

Questions

Can you supply large items like beds or couches?

We can price them, but freight from China is charged by size and weight, so large items cost more to bring in. Tell us the item and your city and we will give you the full price.

Do you provide installation or servicing?

No. We source and supply the equipment. Plan installation, servicing and maintenance with a local biomedical engineer.

Can I order spare parts and accessories?

Yes. Send the part name or number and the equipment it is for.

Surgical instruments in Pakistan

Reusable stainless steel instruments for clinics, operation theatres and dental and veterinary practices. Pakistan's surgical instrument industry is centred in Sialkot, and we source instruments on order from makers in Pakistan or abroad.

Items we source

Surgical scissors

Operating, dissecting (Metzenbaum, Mayo), bandage and suture scissors in straight and curved patterns.

When you ask for a price, tell us: Pattern, length, straight or curved, and quantity.

Forceps

Dissecting and tissue forceps, artery forceps (haemostats) and sponge-holding forceps.

When you ask for a price, tell us: Type and pattern, length, toothed or non-toothed, and quantity.

Needle holders

Mayo-Hegar and other needle holders, with or without tungsten carbide inserts.

When you ask for a price, tell us: Pattern, length and insert type.

Scalpel handles and blades

Reusable scalpel handles and sterile disposable blades in standard numbers.

When you ask for a price, tell us: Handle number and blade numbers (for example 10, 11, 15, 22), and quantity.

Retractors and clamps

Hand-held and self-retaining retractors, towel clips and clamps.

When you ask for a price, tell us: Type, size and quantity.

Instrument sets

Minor surgery, dressing, suturing and delivery sets, in a tray or pouch.

When you ask for a price, tell us: The procedure the set is for, the instruments you need in it, and quantity.

Questions

Are surgical instruments made in Pakistan?

Yes. Sialkot is the main centre of Pakistan's surgical instrument industry. Tell us the pattern and quantity and we will source it.

Can I order instruments in quantity?

Yes. Order individual instruments, full sets or quantities for a hospital department.

Do you sterilise instruments?

No. Reusable instruments must be cleaned and sterilised by your clinic before use, following your infection control procedure.

Questions

Do you keep stock?

No. Items are imported on order. You tell us what you need, we price it in rupees, and once you confirm we source it and deliver it to you.

Who do you supply?

Clinics, hospitals, laboratories and individuals across Pakistan. You can order a single item or a larger quantity for a clinic.

Where are you based?

Islamabad. We supply customers across Pakistan.

How do I get a price?

Fill in the quote form with the item name, model or a link, the quantity and your city. We reply on WhatsApp with a price in rupees.

Are the products DRAP registered?

Many medical devices in Pakistan fall under DRAP's Medical Devices Rules 2017. Ask about a specific item and we will tell you what documentation the supplier provides.

Do you install, calibrate or service equipment?

No. We source and supply equipment. Arrange installation, calibration and servicing with a local biomedical engineer.

CPAP machine price in Pakistan: CPAP vs APAP and masks

Updated November 1, 2026 · 8 min read

Quick answer

CPAP machine price in Pakistan depends mainly on whether the machine gives one fixed pressure (CPAP) or adjusts pressure automatically (APAP), whether it has a heated humidifier and heated tube, its data and comfort features, and the mask you choose. Brand, the exchange rate and quantity also matter. SurgiSource quotes today's price in rupees through its quote form.

A CPAP machine blows a steady stream of air through a hose and mask to keep the airway open during sleep. It is used for obstructive sleep apnoea, and its pressure is set by the patient's healthcare provider. This guide explains CPAP, APAP and bilevel machines, the main mask types, humidifiers and what moves the price in Pakistan. It is an equipment guide, so follow your doctor's advice on whether you need a machine and how it should be set.

What does a CPAP machine do?

A CPAP (continuous positive airway pressure) machine delivers gentle, steady air pressure to keep the airway open during sleep. MedlinePlus, the US National Library of Medicine's health site, explains that the user wears a mask over the nose, or over the nose and mouth, connected by a hose to a small machine beside the bed. The US National Heart, Lung, and Blood Institute (NHLBI) describes CPAP as constant air pressure through the mouth and/or nose.

A complete set has three parts you will price: the machine, the humidifier and tubing, and the mask. Filters, mask cushions and tubes are replaced over time, so they are a running cost.

What is the difference between CPAP, APAP and BiPAP?

CPAP gives one fixed pressure, APAP adjusts the pressure automatically through the night, and BiPAP (bilevel) gives one pressure when breathing in and a lower one when breathing out. These definitions follow the NHLBI's treatment page.

CPAP (fixed pressure)One constant pressure set by the prescriberSimplest type, with the fewest automatic functions
APAP (auto-adjusting)Adjusts the pressure automatically during sleepSome auto machines, such as the F&P SleepStyle, also list fixed pressure

Fisher and Paykel Healthcare (F&P), for example, lists both fixed pressure and auto-adjusting pressure on its SleepStyle machines. Which type and pressure a patient needs is a medical decision, so follow your doctor's advice and bring the prescription when you request a quote.

Which CPAP mask types are there?

CPAP masks differ in how much of the face they cover: full face masks cover the nose and mouth, nasal masks cover or sit under the nose, and nasal pillows seal at the nostrils. F&P's range gives clear examples:

F&P says its masks work with all home PAP machines, and it notes the Evora Nasal may suit people who breathe freely through the nose or want a minimal mask. Mask fit matters as much as the machine. NHLBI advises telling your provider if the mask does not stay on, fits poorly or leaks air, as they may suggest a different mask.

Do you need a heated humidifier and heated tube?

A heated humidifier adds moisture to the air, and many current machines include one. F&P explains that CPAP air can dry the nose and upper airway, and that a heated tube helps prevent rainout, where warm moist air cools and condenses into water in the tube. Its SleepStyle machines have an integrated humidifier, and its ThermoSmart heated tube must be bought separately in some countries.

F&P's care advice for the water chamber is to use distilled water, refill daily, clean the chamber daily, deep clean at least every two weeks and replace the chamber about every six months. Budget for distilled water and spare chambers.

Which features add to the cost of a CPAP machine?

Beyond pressure type and humidifier, comfort and data features add the most cost. Examples from F&P's SleepStyle range include:

  • Expiratory relief: lowers pressure on each breath out.
  • SensAwake: lowers pressure when it detects the user may be waking.
  • Data: Bluetooth, a phone app, USB data download, and on some models built-in cellular connection. The SleepStyle+ Auto lists efficacy and compliance reporting.
  • Auto-altitude adjustment: listed on the SleepStyle+ Auto.
  • Travel bag and compact design.

Ranges change by country. F&P, for example, says it no longer sells the SleepStyle in the United States, so check that the model you are quoted is still supported where you live.

What should you know about used or recalled CPAP machines?

Check any used CPAP machine against recall notices before you buy it. On 14 June 2021, Philips announced a recall of certain CPAP, bilevel and ventilator devices, mostly in the first-generation DreamStation family, because their sound abatement foam (PE-PUR) could break down into particles and release chemicals. Philips said the newer DreamStation 2 CPAP was not affected and ran a repair and replacement programme.

Ask the seller for the exact model and serial number, and check the maker's recall information yourself. For more, see our guide to refurbished vs new medical equipment.

Which comfort problems should you plan for when buying?

Early comfort problems are common, and the right accessories help. MedlinePlus lists feeling closed in, eye irritation, redness over the bridge of the nose, a runny or stuffy nose, and a dry mouth among first-use effects, and notes that many can be helped. NHLBI says a provider may change the mask type, machine type or pressure settings, or add a humidifier. When budgeting, allow for a second mask size or style and a humidifier, and take any health concern to your doctor.

What changes the CPAP machine price in Pakistan?

The price of a CPAP machine in Pakistan is set mainly by pressure type and features, then by mask, brand and import costs. The main factors are:

  • Pressure type: fixed CPAP, auto APAP or bilevel.
  • Humidifier: integrated, separate or none, and a heated tube.
  • Comfort and data features: expiratory relief, wake detection, apps and connectivity.
  • Mask: full face, nasal or nasal pillows, and spare cushions.
  • Brand and model: including local service and parts support.
  • New or used: and the recall status of used units.
  • Exchange rate, freight and duty: CPAP machines and masks are imported, so the rupee rate on the order day matters.
  • Quantity: orders for a sleep clinic or hospital can change the unit cost.

Because these move often, at the time of writing (November 2026) SurgiSource does not publish fixed prices. We quote the price on the day you ask.

What should you ask before ordering a CPAP machine?

  1. Is it fixed CPAP, auto APAP or bilevel, and does it match the prescription?
  2. Is a humidifier included, and is a heated tube included or extra?
  3. Which mask type and size come with it, and can you change size?
  4. What data does it record, and how do you share it with your doctor?
  5. Does the power supply suit your mains, and can it run from a UPS during load shedding?
  6. For a used unit, what are the model and serial number, and is it affected by any recall?
  7. What warranty applies? See our guide to medical equipment warranty in Pakistan.

Frequently asked questions

What is the difference between CPAP and APAP?

A CPAP machine gives one fixed pressure all night, while an APAP machine adjusts the pressure automatically during sleep. Which one a patient needs is a medical decision, so follow your doctor's advice.

Which CPAP mask is best?

No single mask suits everyone. Full face masks cover the nose and mouth, nasal masks cover or sit under the nose, and nasal pillows seal at the nostrils, and fit, comfort and your provider's advice decide the right choice.

Do I need a humidifier with a CPAP machine?

Many machines include one because CPAP air can dry the nose and airway. A heated tube also helps stop water collecting in the hose, so ask whether one is included.

Is it safe to buy a used CPAP machine?

Check the model and serial number against recall notices first, such as the 2021 Philips recall that mainly affected first-generation DreamStation devices. We suggest new masks and tubing for each new user.

Interacoustics vs MAICO audiometers: how the ranges compare

Updated November 1, 2026 · 8 min read

Quick answer

Interacoustics and MAICO are both audiometer brands in the Diagnostics business of the Danish group Demant. MAICO focuses on screening and portable audiometers such as the MA 25, MA 28 and MA 42. Interacoustics covers screening through full clinical systems, including the AD226, AD629, AC40 and Equinox Evo. Choose by the tests you need, not the brand.

Interacoustics and MAICO are two of the audiometer brands Pakistani clinics, ENT practices and hearing aid centres ask about most. They often turn up side by side in quotes, and they are more closely related than many buyers realise. This guide compares the two ranges model by model, using the makers' own product pages and specification sheets, so you can match a device to the work you actually do.

Are Interacoustics and MAICO the same company?

No, they are separate brands, but both belong to the Diagnostics business of the Danish hearing health group Demant. Demant's history page says it bought MAICO in 1995 as part of its purchase of the hearing aid maker Bernafon, and that this laid the foundation for its diagnostics division. It bought Interacoustics in 2000.

  • Interacoustics was founded in 1967 in Assens, Denmark, by Pierre Honoré and Michael Davis. In 2025 it moved into a new headquarters in Middelfart, Denmark.
  • MAICO was founded by Leland A. Watson in 1937. It has offices in Berlin and Minneapolis and says its devices are sold in 112 countries.

The two brands share a parent, but they design and sell their own products, use their own distributors and publish their own specification sheets.

How do the Interacoustics and MAICO audiometer ranges compare?

MAICO concentrates on screening and portable audiometers, while Interacoustics covers screening through to full clinical systems. MAICO's About page describes its work as developing easy-to-use hearing screening solutions. Which screening audiometers does each brand offer?

MAICO offers more screening options, from a palm-sized screener up to portable air conduction audiometers, while Interacoustics offers the AS608 and the Luna headset.

  • MAICO MA 1: a handheld air conduction screener powered by 2 AA batteries. It tests at 500, 1000, 2000 and 4000 Hz from 15 to 50 dB HL, and comes with a DD45 headphone or an optional DD65 v2 noise-excluding headset.
  • MAICO MA 25: an air conduction audiometer weighing 980 g, with 11 frequencies from 125 Hz to 8000 Hz and levels from -10 to 100 dB HL. It runs on 3 AA batteries, a power supply or USB. The MA 25e version adds an automated Hughson-Westlake test and talk forward. MAICO says it had sold 36,802 MA 25 units by 2022.
  • MAICO MA 27: similar test range to the MA 25 in a case with a handle and a storage compartment for the headphones.
  • Interacoustics AS608: a portable screener for schools, industry and primary care. It weighs less than 1.6 kg and runs on 3 AA batteries.

Which diagnostic audiometers does each brand offer?

For diagnostic work, the MAICO MA 42 competes most directly with the Interacoustics AD226, AD528 and AD629.

  • MAICO MA 42: a two-channel air, bone and speech audiometer weighing 1.5 kg. It offers optional high frequency testing up to 16,000 Hz, special tests such as Stenger and SISI, and an internal database for at least 500 patients.
  • Interacoustics AD226: routine air and bone conduction threshold audiometry, with more tests unlocked through an extended software option. It weighs 1.3 kg, measures 30 x 23 x 9 cm, stores 500 patients and can run on AA batteries.
  • Interacoustics AD528: a compact diagnostic audiometer for air conduction, bone conduction and speech audiometry, with a 5 inch display.
  • Interacoustics AD629: a hybrid diagnostic audiometer that works on its own or as a PC-based audiometer. Its 2024 specification sheet lists 125 Hz to 8 kHz as standard, with optional high frequency testing from 8 to 20 kHz.

What if you need a full clinical audiometer?

If you need a full clinical test battery, Interacoustics is the brand to look at, because MAICO does not list a clinical-level audiometer. The Interacoustics AC40 is a two-channel clinical audiometer with high frequency testing up to 20 kHz, speech-in-noise tests and masking level difference testing. The Equinox Evo is a PC-based two-channel clinical audiometer that also reaches 20 kHz. Interacoustics notes the Equinox Evo is not available in all countries, so check with the distributor first.

Which brand suits schools, camps and workplace testing?

Both brands make portable screeners for schools and camps, but only MAICO lists a dedicated multi-person occupational system. The Tremetrics RA660 is a PC-controlled industrial audiometer that can test up to 8 people at the same time, weighs 500 g and includes a Bio-Acoustic Simulator for daily listening checks. For children, the MAICO Pilot Test combines pure tone screening with a picture-pointing speech test, and Interacoustics offers the PA5 free field audiometer and VRA for young children.

How do their PC-based audiometers and software compare?

Both brands offer PC-connected audiometers and link to the common practice databases. The MAICO MA 33 runs over USB from a PC or notebook, covers air and bone conduction, and includes SISI, Stenger and automatic Hughson-Westlake tests. MAICO Sessions software connects to OtoAccess or Noah databases. On the Interacoustics side, Callisto is a USB audiometer with hearing aid fitting options, the AD629 can run standalone or PC-based, and OtoAccess is its integration software. MAICO notes the MA 33 and MA 42 are not sold in North, Central or South America, which shows that availability differs by region.

Which headphones and bone conductors come with them?

Most of these audiometers use RadioEar transducers, and the exact set depends on the model. The Interacoustics AD629 specification lists a DD45 headset and B71 bone conductor as included, with IP30 insert phones, TDH39, DD450 and DD65v2 headsets and a B81 bone conductor as options. The MAICO MA 1 ships with a DD45 headphone. Each transducer needs its own reference values, so any change of headset must be set up on the audiometer. Our DD45 vs DD450 guide explains the difference between the two most common headsets.

What should you check before buying either brand in Pakistan?

Check the exact model, its options and the support around it, not just the brand name.

  1. Test range: air only, air and bone, speech, masking and high frequency. Optional features often need a paid software option.
  2. Transducers: which headset, bone conductor and insert phones are included.
  3. Power: mains only, or batteries for field work.
  4. Data: internal storage, printer and PC software.
  5. Availability: MAICO says specifications may vary by country, and Interacoustics says some products are not sold everywhere, so ask for the datasheet that applies to your unit.
  6. Calibration and service: who will calibrate the audiometer when it arrives and at the intervals the maker sets. Our calibration guide explains why this matters.

Frequently asked questions

Is MAICO owned by Interacoustics?

No. Both are separate brands owned by the Demant group. Demant bought MAICO in 1995 and Interacoustics in 2000.

Which MAICO audiometer is closest to the Interacoustics AD629?

The MAICO MA 42 is the nearest match. Both are portable diagnostic audiometers with air, bone and speech testing and optional high frequency testing, though the AD629 option reaches 20 kHz and the MA 42 option reaches 16,000 Hz.

Does MAICO make a clinical audiometer like the AC40?

MAICO's audiometer list does not include a clinical-level device at the time of writing (November 2026). For a full clinical test battery, Interacoustics offers the AC40 and Equinox Evo.

Which audiometer is best for school screening?

Both brands make portable screeners, such as the MAICO MA 25 and the Interacoustics AS608, which run on AA batteries. MAICO also offers the Pilot Test for children.

Do these audiometers need calibration after delivery?

Yes. Any audiometer must be calibrated with the exact headset and bone conductor it will be used with. Arrange this with a qualified service engineer.

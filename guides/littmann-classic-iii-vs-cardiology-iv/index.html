Littmann Classic III vs Cardiology IV stethoscope: what is different?

Updated November 1, 2026 · 7 min read

Quick answer

The Littmann Cardiology IV has a chestpiece 40% larger and a bell 60% deeper than the Classic III, a double lumen tube instead of single lumen, and a 7-year warranty instead of 5 years. Both are 69 cm long, use tunable diaphragms on a dual-sided chestpiece and are not recommended for neonates.

The Littmann Classic III and Cardiology IV are two of the most requested stethoscopes among doctors, nurses and medical students in Pakistan. They look alike at first glance, but Littmann positions them differently. This guide compares them using Littmann's own product pages, so you can decide which one fits the work you do.

What is the main difference between Classic III and Cardiology IV?

The main difference is the chestpiece and tube design. Littmann says the Cardiology IV chestpiece is 40% larger and its bell 60% deeper than the Classic III. Littmann lists the Cardiology IV tube as double lumen and the Classic III as single lumen. The Cardiology IV also carries a longer warranty, 7 years against 5 years.

Littmann's pages describe both models as lighter than other stethoscopes but do not give a weight in grams, so check the spec sheet in the box if weight matters to you.

Both pages say the stethoscopes are assembled in the USA with globally sourced materials. Both come with soft-sealing eartips and an adjustable headset. Littmann describes the Cardiology IV eartips as bulbous and shaped to conform to individual ears, and the eartips on both models snap on and can be removed. On the Classic III, Littmann says you change the headset tension by squeezing the eartubes together or pulling them apart, and the box includes large and small eartips and a non-chill bell sleeve.

What does a larger chestpiece and deeper bell change?

A larger chestpiece and deeper bell are Littmann's design choices for the Cardiology IV, which it places in its cardiology and high-performance group. Littmann lists settings such as the emergency department, ICU, cardiac ICU and step-down units for the Cardiology IV. The Classic III is aimed at physical assessment, patient monitoring and general diagnosis by doctors, nurses, clinicians and students.

What do single lumen and double lumen mean?

A lumen is an internal channel in the tube. Littmann lists the Classic III as single lumen and the Cardiology IV as double lumen, which means the Cardiology IV tube carries two internal sound channels inside one outer tube. Both tubes use Littmann's Next Generation Tubing. Littmann notes that PVC tubing can stiffen after long contact with oils from the skin.

Is the tunable diaphragm the same on both models?

Both models use Littmann's tunable diaphragm. Light pressure on the chestpiece brings out low-frequency sounds, and firmer pressure brings out higher-frequency sounds, without turning the chestpiece over. Both chestpieces are dual-sided. On the Classic III you rotate the chestpiece to open either side, and Littmann notes that only the opened side transmits sound. Littmann describes the Cardiology IV chestpiece as dual-sided with an open or closed bell, and it credits 3M with inventing the tunable diaphragm technology used on both.

On the Classic III, the removable diaphragm covers the open bell to keep out debris, and the non-chill bell sleeve can be taken off for cleaning. These small parts can be lost, so ask whether spare diaphragms, rims and eartips are available for the model you choose.

Which Littmann model suits students, nurses and general practice?

Littmann lists the Classic III for doctors, nurses, clinicians and healthcare students, and the Cardiology IV for seasoned clinicians in acute and cardiac settings. Neither page ranks one as better for every user. A student or a general practitioner who mainly needs routine assessment may find the Classic III fits that description. How do you tell the models apart by catalog number?

Each colour and finish has its own catalog number, which is printed on the box and listed on Littmann's product pages. Matching it is a quick way to confirm that what arrives is the model you ordered. Examples from Littmann's US site:

Littmann also offers satin-finish tubing on select models. It lists Champagne Rose and Marine Blue for the Classic III, and Alabaster and Midnight Blue for the Cardiology IV.

Can either stethoscope be used on babies?

Littmann does not recommend either model for neonates. For newborns it points to the Littmann Classic II Infant. The Cardiology IV is listed for adult and pediatric patients.

How do warranty and genuine stock affect the choice?

The warranty only helps if the stethoscope is genuine and the claim is accepted. Littmann says its stethoscopes are sold through authorised dealers who set their own prices. Because dealers set their own prices, the same model can cost different amounts from different sellers. Ask any seller where the stock comes from and how a warranty claim would be handled. Keep the box, the catalog number and the invoice together, as you may need them if you make a claim later. Our guide on spotting a fake Littmann lists what to check on arrival.

How should you clean and look after either model?

Littmann's care advice is the same for both models. Clean with 70% isopropyl alcohol wipes. Do not immerse the stethoscope, do not sterilise it, do not clean it with hand sanitiser and do not store it in extreme heat. Both contain metal and are not for use in an MRI room. Laser engraving is acceptable, but Littmann does not recommend scratch engraving. See our guide on stethoscope care for a full routine.

Frequently asked questions

Is the Cardiology IV louder than the Classic III?

Littmann says the Cardiology IV has a 40% larger chestpiece and a 60% deeper bell than the Classic III. Which one sounds better is a personal judgement, so try both if you can.

What is the warranty on Littmann Classic III and Cardiology IV?

Littmann lists 5 years for the Classic III and 7 years for the Cardiology IV.

Are the Classic III and Cardiology IV the same length?

Yes. Littmann lists both at 69 cm (27.17 in) overall.

Is the Classic III suitable for medical students?

Littmann lists the Classic III for doctors, nurses, clinicians and healthcare students.

Can I use a Littmann Cardiology IV on newborns?

Littmann does not recommend the Cardiology IV or Classic III for neonates. It suggests the Classic II Infant for newborns.

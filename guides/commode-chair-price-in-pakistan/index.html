Commode chair price in Pakistan: types and what to check

Updated November 1, 2026 · 7 min read

Quick answer

Commode chair prices in Pakistan depend mainly on the type: a folding steel commode is the simplest, while drop-arm, wheeled and bariatric commodes cost more. Before ordering, check the weight capacity, seat height range, width between the arms, bucket size and whether the frame can also be used over a toilet.

A commode chair lets a person use the toilet beside the bed or in a room without a bathroom. Families caring for someone at home, care homes and hospitals all use them. This guide explains the main types of commode chair, the measurements to take, the weight ratings to look for and the international standard that covers them. It does not replace advice from a doctor, nurse or occupational therapist about what the user needs.

What is a commode chair?

A commode chair is a chair with a toilet seat and a removable bucket or pan underneath, used as a toilet away from the bathroom. Most have arms for support when sitting down and standing up. The international standard for assistive products for personal hygiene, ISO 17966, lists commode chairs under ISO 9999 code 09 12 03 and notes that this covers both mobile and static products.

A folding steel commode is the basic type. Drive says its 11148-1 has a grey powder-coated welded steel frame with plastic arms and folds flat for storage and transport.

What is a drop-arm commode and who is it for?

A drop-arm commode has arms that lower out of the way so the user can slide sideways onto the seat from a bed or wheelchair. Drive describes the drop arm on its 11125PSKD-1 as making transfers easier, and the drop arms on its wheeled 11120SV-1F as allowing easier lateral (sideways) transfers. Ask the person's nurse or therapist which transfer method is safe for them, and choose the chair to match.

Can a commode chair be used over a toilet?

Some can, because the frame without the bucket also works as a raised toilet seat or a toilet safety frame. Drive says its ProBasics Steel Drop-Arm Commode (BSDAC) can be set up as a bedside commode, a toilet safety frame or a raised toilet seat. Sellers often call this a 3-in-1 commode. Not every commode does this, so check that the product page lists these uses and that the frame fits around your toilet. Measure the width of the toilet bowl area and the height of the toilet before ordering.

What should you measure before buying a commode chair?

Measure the user, the room and any doorways the chair must pass through. A short list:

  • Seat height: adjustable legs let you match the seat to the user. The 11125PSKD-1 adjusts from about 48 to 58 cm and the 11117N-1 from about 39 to 56 cm.
  • Seat width and width between arms: the user should fit comfortably between the arms. The 11125PSKD-1 has 19 inches (about 48 cm) between the arms and the 11120SV-1F has 18.5 inches (about 47 cm).
  • Overall width: needed for doorways and bathrooms. The 11120SV-1F measures 22.25 inches (about 56.5 cm) across the outside of the arms, and the bariatric 11117N-1 is 29.25 inches (about 74 cm) between the legs.
  • Bucket size: Drive buckets range from 7.5 qt (about 7.1 L) on the standard folding model to 12 qt (about 11.4 L) on the bariatric model.

What weight capacity does a commode chair need?

The rated capacity should be comfortably above the user's body weight. Standard steel commodes in the Drive range are rated 350 lb (about 159 kg), and the bariatric 11117N-1 is rated 650 lb (about 295 kg). If a page does not state the weight capacity, as with the 11125PSKD-1 product page, ask the supplier for it in writing before you order.

Should a commode chair have wheels?

Wheels help when the user needs to be moved between rooms or over a toilet, but a static commode is simpler and has fewer parts. Drive's wheeled 11120SV-1F also has footrests, a padded vinyl seat and a collection pan that slides out from the back. Its product page does not list the caster size or lock type, so ask for both. Check that the casters lock, so the chair stays still while the user gets on and off.

Which standard covers commode chairs?

The international standard is ISO 17966, which sets requirements and test methods for assistive products for personal hygiene. Its scope includes commode chairs, raised toilet seats, toilet arm supports and shower chairs. It covers safety and performance in normal use and foreseeable misuse, and limits the force needed to operate some controls. Standards Norway lists the 2016 edition as withdrawn and replaced by ISO 17966:2026 at the time of writing, so ask which edition a product was tested to.

What spare parts and accessories are available for commode chairs?

The usual spares are replacement buckets and lids, and disposable commode liners. Drive lists a 12 qt commode bucket with handle and lid, a bariatric bucket and cover, and absorbent commode liners as separate items, including a folding commode sold as a combo with liners. Buckets are made to fit a particular frame, so give the supplier the exact model when ordering spares. Care homes and hospitals that use several chairs may want to order spare buckets and liners with the first order.

What changes the price of a commode chair in Pakistan?

The price depends on the type, frame, features and brand. A basic folding steel commode is the simplest. Drop arms, wheels with locks, padded seats, footrests and bariatric frames each add cost. Other factors:

  • Bucket and lid quality: buckets with a cover, splash shield and handle.
  • Brand: branded imports cost more than generic frames.
  • Exchange rate and freight: wheeled and bariatric commodes are bulky to ship.
  • Quantity: hospitals and care homes ordering several chairs may get a different unit price.

Frequently asked questions

What is a 3-in-1 commode?

It is a commode whose frame can be used as a bedside commode, a raised toilet seat or a toilet safety frame. Drive's ProBasics BSDAC, for example, lists all three set-ups.

What is a drop-arm commode used for?

The arms lower out of the way so the user can slide sideways onto the seat from a bed or wheelchair. Ask the nurse or therapist whether this transfer method suits the user.

How much weight can a commode chair hold?

Standard steel commodes in the Drive range are rated 350 lb (about 159 kg), and bariatric models such as the 11117N-1 are rated 650 lb (about 295 kg). Always check the rating of the exact model.

How big is a commode bucket?

Sizes vary by model. Drive's standard folding commode has a 7.5 qt (about 7.1 L) bucket and its bariatric folding commode a 12 qt (about 11.4 L) bucket.

Is there a standard for commode chairs?

Yes. ISO 17966 sets requirements and test methods for assistive products for personal hygiene, including commode chairs, and a 2026 edition has replaced the 2016 edition according to Standards Norway.

A digital stethoscope looks like a normal stethoscope but has electronics in the chestpiece that make body sounds louder and cut background noise. Some models also record sounds, stream them to a phone or show an ECG trace. This guide explains what digital stethoscopes do, how the main models compare, who tends to need one and what changes the price in Pakistan. It is a buying guide for equipment and does not cover how to interpret what you hear.

What is a digital stethoscope?

A digital stethoscope, also called an electronic stethoscope, is a stethoscope that turns body sounds into an electrical signal, amplifies it and plays it through the ear tips or headphones. The US Food and Drug Administration (FDA) defines an electronic stethoscope as an electrically amplified device used to project the sounds of the heart, arteries, veins and other internal organs. It places electronic stethoscopes in Class II (special controls), while a manual acoustic stethoscope is Class I.

Ask whether a model has clearance or approval from the regulator in its home market, and check how it is treated in Pakistan.

What can a digital stethoscope do that an acoustic one cannot?

A digital stethoscope can make sounds louder, reduce background noise, filter for different sound ranges and, on many models, record and share sounds. The main features are:

  • Amplification: Eko states up to 40 times for the 3M Littmann CORE, and Thinklabs states 100 times for the Thinklabs One.
  • Active noise cancellation: listed for the Littmann CORE and Eko CORE 500.
  • Filters: Thinklabs lists 5 filter options to focus on different sounds.
  • Analog mode: the Littmann CORE and the CORE Digital Attachment can switch between normal acoustic listening and amplified listening.
  • Recording and sharing: through a phone app, useful for teaching and second opinions.
  • ECG: the Eko CORE 500 adds a 3-lead ECG, with a 1-lead trace shown on its screen.
  • Software analysis: Eko offers FDA-cleared software that flags suspected murmurs and atrial fibrillation, through a paid membership.

Who needs a digital stethoscope?

A digital stethoscope is most useful for clinicians who struggle to hear through an acoustic model, who work in noisy places or who need to record and share sounds. Typical buyers include:

  • Clinicians with hearing loss: Thinklabs lists hard-of-hearing users as a use case, and Eko says the CORE 500 can pair with some hearing aids, though compatibility varies by hearing aid model, so check with your audiologist.
  • Emergency and ambulance teams: amplification and noise cancellation help in loud settings.
  • Teachers and students: recording, playback and on-screen sound views help in teaching.
  • Telemedicine services: Thinklabs states that the One connects to most telemedicine systems.

For routine use in a quiet clinic, a good acoustic stethoscope may be enough. Our comparison of the Littmann Classic III and Cardiology IV covers the acoustic side.

How long does the battery last in daily use?

Battery life varies a lot between models, and makers measure it in different ways, so compare like with like. Eko gives the Littmann CORE up to 80 hours of regular clinical use. For the CORE 500, which also runs a screen and ECG, it gives 300 minutes of continuous use or 60 hours of clinical use. Thinklabs gives the One a capacity of 100 to 120 patients per charge with normal use.

Before ordering, ask how the unit is charged, whether a spare charging cable is included, and whether the model can still be used acoustically when the battery is flat.

Is recording patient sounds a privacy concern?

Yes, recordings of heart and lung sounds are patient data, so treat them with the same care as other clinical records. Eko states that its app lets users record, share and organise exam recordings, and that an independent auditor has reviewed its security controls under the SOC 2 Type II framework. Before using any recording or sharing feature, agree a policy in your clinic on patient consent, where files are stored and who can see them.

What changes the price of a digital stethoscope in Pakistan?

The price depends on the model, its features, any paid software membership, the quantity and the exchange rate on the day of order. SurgiSource does not publish fixed prices.

  • Model and features: an ECG model with a display costs more than an amplification-only model or an attachment.
  • Software membership: Eko's analysis features need a paid Eko+ membership on top of the device.
  • Accessories: spare ear tips, charging cables and cases.
  • Quantity: orders for a department or teaching institution may get a lower unit price.
  • Exchange rate: imported stock is priced against foreign currency on the day of order.

Does the AI feature work in Pakistan?

Not necessarily, so ask before you pay for it. Eko states that Eko+ gives access to its AI software only in countries where Eko Health has regulatory approval, and that the AI features are intended for licensed healthcare professionals. Without the membership, Eko says the app still offers basic features such as live sound visualisation, wireless listening and recording with playback. Confirm availability in Pakistan at the time you order, because coverage can change.

What should you check before ordering a digital stethoscope?

  1. Amplification level and whether an analog mode is included.
  2. Battery life and the charging method, including the cable and plug.
  3. Phone app support for your phone, and whether the app is available in Pakistan.
  4. Hearing aid or headphone pairing, if you need it.
  5. Warranty terms and who handles repairs. See our guide to medical equipment warranty in Pakistan.
  6. That the unit is genuine, with serial numbers and original packaging.

How do you look after a digital stethoscope?

Look after it like an acoustic stethoscope, but keep it charged and keep liquids away from the electronics. Eko describes the CORE 500 as scratch- and splash-proof and the Littmann CORE as water-resistant, which is not the same as waterproof. Follow the maker's cleaning instructions for the chestpiece and ear tips. Our guide on how to clean and care for a stethoscope covers the basics.

Frequently asked questions

What is the difference between a digital and an acoustic stethoscope?

A digital stethoscope converts body sounds into an electrical signal and amplifies them, and many models also cancel noise and record. An acoustic stethoscope carries sound through tubing with no battery.

How much louder is a digital stethoscope?

It depends on the model. Eko states up to 40 times for the 3M Littmann CORE and Eko CORE 500, and Thinklabs states 100 times for the Thinklabs One.

Can I turn my existing stethoscope into a digital one?

Yes, with an attachment. Eko's CORE Digital Attachment fits many acoustic models from Littmann, ADC, Welch Allyn, MDF and Medline; check your exact model on the compatibility list.

Do digital stethoscopes help people with hearing loss?

They can help. Thinklabs lists hard-of-hearing clinicians as a use case, and Eko says the CORE 500 can pair with some hearing aids, though compatibility varies, so check with your audiologist.

Is the Eko AI murmur detection available in Pakistan?

Eko says its AI is offered only in countries where it has regulatory approval and needs a paid Eko+ membership. Confirm availability in Pakistan before paying for it.

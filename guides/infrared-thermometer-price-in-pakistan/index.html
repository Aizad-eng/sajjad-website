Infrared thermometer price in Pakistan: forehead vs ear models

Updated November 1, 2026 · 8 min read

Quick answer

The price of an infrared thermometer in Pakistan depends on whether it is a forehead or ear model, the brand, the features and the rupee exchange rate on the day of import. Forehead models need no consumables, while many ear models need a new lens filter for each reading. SurgiSource quotes today's rupee price on request.

Infrared thermometers take a temperature in a second or two by reading the heat given off by the body. In Pakistan they are bought by families, schools, clinics and pharmacies. The two common types are forehead (non-contact) models and ear (tympanic) models. They are used differently, cost different amounts to run and suit different settings. This guide compares the two and explains what changes the price. It covers the equipment only. If a reading worries you, follow your doctor's advice.

How does an infrared thermometer measure temperature?

An infrared thermometer measures temperature by sensing the thermal radiation (heat) given off by the body, without needing to stay in contact for long. The ASTM standard for these devices covers two uses: estimating internal body temperature from the heat given off inside the ear canal, and measuring skin temperature without contact.

The US Food and Drug Administration (FDA) explains that non-contact infrared thermometers (NCITs) measure surface temperature, usually at the forehead. An ear thermometer has a probe that sits in the ear canal. Its tip is covered with a disposable lens filter.

Forehead or ear: which infrared thermometer should you buy?

Choose a forehead model when you want quick, no-touch checks on many people, and an ear model when you want a reading from inside the ear canal and do not mind buying lens filters. What changes the price of an infrared thermometer in Pakistan?

The price of an infrared thermometer in Pakistan depends mainly on the type, the brand and the features. Units are imported, so the rupee price is set on the day of the order. The main factors are:

  • Type: ear and forehead models are built differently. Ear models also need lens filters, which is an ongoing cost.
  • Brand: branded models from makers such as Braun and Omron usually cost more than generic models. Both of these makers publish accuracy figures for their models.
  • Features: memory, colour fever indicators, night lights, Bluetooth apps and extra modes (such as room or surface temperature) add to the price.
  • Accessories: spare lens filters, batteries and storage cases.
  • Exchange rate and import costs: the rupee rate, freight and duties change the landed price. Duty and tax rules change, so check the current rule.
  • Quantity: schools, offices and clinics buying several units may get a different unit price.

Which accuracy standards should an infrared thermometer meet?

Look for a thermometer that states it meets ASTM E1965 or ISO 80601-2-56, the two standards the FDA lists for these devices. ASTM E1965-98 (reaffirmed in 2023) is the specification for infrared thermometers used for intermittent checks of patient temperature. It sets limits for laboratory accuracy and requires the maker to work out and disclose clinical accuracy. ISO 80601-2-56:2017 covers basic safety and essential performance of clinical thermometers.

Braun's manual states that the ThermoScan meets ASTM E1965-98 and notes that ASTM's laboratory accuracy limit for infrared thermometers between 37 and 39 °C is ±0.2 °C. It also gives a clinical repeatability of ±0.14 °C. Ask for these numbers when you compare models. A box that only says "accurate" without figures tells you little. What affects the reading on a forehead thermometer?

Distance, angle, room conditions and the state of the forehead all affect a forehead reading. The FDA lists these points for non-contact infrared thermometers:

  • Hold the device at the distance the maker specifies, which differs by model.
  • Keep the sensor perpendicular (straight on) to the forehead and the person still.
  • Avoid drafts, direct sunlight and radiant heat. Use in a room of about 16 to 40 °C with relative humidity below 85 percent.
  • Leave the thermometer in the room for 10 to 30 minutes before use so it can adjust.
  • The forehead should be clean, dry and uncovered. Headbands, heavy clothing and cosmetic wipes can change the reading.

Most NCITs should not be put in water. Clean them as the manual says. Our guide on how to use an infrared thermometer explains the steps.

What affects the reading on an ear thermometer?

Probe position, a used or missing lens filter, a dirty lens and earwax are the main things that affect an ear reading. Braun's manual for the ThermoScan 7+ connect says:

  • Use a new, clean lens filter every time. The thermometer must be used only with genuine Braun LF 40 filters.
  • The ear should be free of blockages or heavy earwax.
  • The right and left ears may read differently, so use the same ear each time.
  • Wait 20 minutes after very hot or cold surroundings, or after wearing a hearing aid or headphones.
  • Do not put the thermometer in water.

Braun's manual also says the unit is set at the factory and does not need periodic re-adjustment when used as instructed.

What running costs come with each type?

An ear thermometer has the higher running cost, because it needs a new lens filter for each reading, while a forehead thermometer mainly needs batteries. For a clinic or school taking many readings a day, filter costs add up quickly. Ask for the price of filter refills and check that they are easy to source before choosing an ear model. Battery type matters too: the Braun model above uses two AA cells and the Omron model one CR2032 coin cell, so keep the right spares.

Frequently asked questions

What is the price of an infrared thermometer in Pakistan?

There is no fixed price, because infrared thermometers are imported and priced on the day of order. The type, brand, features, accessories, quantity and the rupee exchange rate all change it. SurgiSource quotes today's price in rupees through its quote form.

Is an ear thermometer more accurate than a forehead thermometer?

Accuracy depends on the model and on correct use. Compare the stated laboratory accuracy of each model and check that it meets ASTM E1965 or ISO 80601-2-56.

Do ear thermometers need lens filters?

Many do. Braun's ThermoScan 7+ connect needs a new genuine LF 40 lens filter for each reading, which is an ongoing cost.

Why does my forehead thermometer give different readings?

Distance, angle, drafts, sunlight, a sweaty or covered forehead and moving between rooms can all change the reading. The FDA advises letting the device sit in the room for 10 to 30 minutes first.

Can an infrared thermometer be washed?

Most should not be put in water. Clean it only as the maker's manual describes.

Omron nebulizer models compared: NE-C28, NE-C101, NE-C803 and NE-U100

Updated November 1, 2026 · 7 min read

Quick answer

Omron's NE-C28, NE-C101 and NE-C803 are compressor nebulizers, while the MicroAir NE-U100 is a battery-powered mesh nebulizer. On Omron's own pages the NE-C28 lists about 3 µm particles at about 0.4 ml/min and weighs 1.9 kg. The NE-U100 lists 5 µm at 0.25 ml/min and weighs about 120 g without batteries.

Omron sells several nebulizers in Asia, and buyers in Pakistan often ask how the models differ. This guide compares four of them using only the figures Omron publishes on its Asia Pacific websites: the NE-C28, NE-C101 and NE-C803 compressor models and the MicroAir NE-U100 mesh model. It covers equipment only. The medicine, dose and how often to use it are decided by your doctor.

Which Omron nebulizer models are usually compared?

The four models in this guide cover Omron's main styles. The NE-C28 and NE-C101 are tabletop compressor units for home use. The NE-C803 is a much smaller compressor unit. The NE-U100, sold as the MicroAir, is a pocket-sized mesh nebulizer that runs on two AA batteries or an optional AC adapter.

What is the difference between a compressor and a mesh nebulizer?

A compressor nebulizer uses a pump to push air through a cup of liquid medicine and turn it into a mist. A mesh nebulizer pushes the liquid through a fine vibrating mesh instead. Omron says the NE-U100 works at any angle, even when tilted or when the user is lying down, and that its mesh design makes it virtually noiseless. Our guide to mesh nebulizers in Pakistan explains the mesh type in more depth.

What do particle size and nebulization rate mean when comparing?

Particle size, given as MMAD (mass median aerodynamic diameter), describes the typical size of the mist droplets. Nebulization rate is how much liquid the unit turns into mist each minute. Omron links the NE-C28's high rate to fast treatment and short inhalation time. On Omron's figures the NE-C28 has the highest listed rate at about 0.4 ml/min, and the NE-U100 the lowest at 0.25 ml/min. Which droplet size suits a given medicine is a medical question, so follow your doctor's advice and the medicine's instructions.

Omron also lists an aerosol output and an aerosol output rate for its compressor models, separately from the nebulization rate. For example, the NE-C28 lists an aerosol output rate of 0.06 ml/min against a nebulization rate of about 0.4 ml/min. The two figures describe different things, so compare the same measure across models and do not mix one model's nebulization rate with another model's aerosol output rate. Where a figure is missing from Omron's page, ask for the instruction manual of the exact unit.

Which Omron model is described for frequent use?

Omron describes the NE-C28 as ideal for frequent and intensive use, with a powerful compressor and a high nebulization rate. It also uses Omron's Virtual Valve Technology (V.V.T.), which Omron links to reduced medication wastage. The NE-C803 page also mentions virtual valve technology. The NE-C101 page highlights that it is easy to use and easy to clean and maintain. Omron lists both the NE-C28 and NE-C101 as suitable for home use.

The NE-C101 has the largest listed medication capacity of the four at 12 ml, and it is the only compressor model here whose box Omron lists with a nosepiece. The NE-C803 is the quietest compressor model on Omron's figures, at below 45 dB.

Which Omron nebulizer is easiest to carry?

On Omron's listed weights, the NE-U100 is the lightest at about 120 g without batteries, followed by the NE-C803 at 0.18 kg. The NE-C101 weighs 1.05 kg and the NE-C28 1.9 kg. For travel or power cuts, battery operation is the main advantage of the NE-U100. Omron lists its power use at about 1.2 W, and the optional AC adapter is rated for 100-240 V at 50-60 Hz. Omron gives an operating range of 10 to 40 °C and 30 to 85% relative humidity for the NE-U100, which is worth noting for use in a hot room or a car during summer. What comes in the box and which parts need replacing?

Omron lists different accessories for each model:

  • NE-C28: nebulizer kit, mouthpiece, adult and child PVC masks, 200 cm air tube, 5 spare air filters and a carrying bag.
  • NE-C101: nebulizer kit, mouthpiece, nosepiece, adult and child PVC masks, 100 cm air tube and 2 spare air filters.
  • NE-U100: medication container, mesh cap, mask adapter, mouthpiece, adult and child PVC masks, 2 AA batteries and a soft pouch.

Air filters on compressor units and the mesh cap on the NE-U100 are parts that wear. Omron sells the NE-U100 mesh cap (part NEB-MC-10E), mask adapter (NEB-MSA-10E) and masks as separate accessories. Its shop lists the same adult and child PVC masks for both the NE-C101 and the NE-U100. Ask whether spare parts can be ordered before you choose a model. Cleaning matters for every type, and our guide on how to clean a nebulizer covers the routine.

Why can specifications differ between countries?

Omron publishes separate regional websites, and box contents, plugs and model suffixes can differ by market. The figures in this guide come from Omron's Philippines and Singapore pages and Omron's Asia Pacific online shop. Before ordering, check the full model code and the instruction manual for the unit actually being supplied. Omron's pages also tell you to refer to the manual for full details, and its NE-C803 page links to the manual directly. Storage conditions are another detail that varies by model. For the NE-U100, Omron lists storage and transport at -20 to +60 °C and 10 to 90% relative humidity, without condensation.

What should you check before ordering an Omron nebulizer?

  1. Compressor or mesh, based on where and how often it will be used.
  2. Whether you need battery use away from a socket, and how many hours of use you need between battery changes.
  3. Masks included for both adult and child users.
  4. Availability of spare filters, kits or mesh caps, and the part codes for them.
  5. Voltage and plug type for Pakistan.
  6. Warranty terms and who handles them.

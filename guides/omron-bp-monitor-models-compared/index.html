Omron BP monitor models compared: HEM-7120, HEM-7121, HEM-7156, HEM-7361T and more

Updated November 1, 2026 · 8 min read

Quick answer

Omron BP monitor models differ mainly in memory, cuff, connectivity and extra checks. The HEM-7120 and HEM-8712 keep only the last reading, the HEM-7121 stores 30, the HEM-7156 stores 60 with an IntelliWrap cuff, T versions add Bluetooth, and the HEM-7361T adds an AFib indicator with 100 readings for each of two users.

Omron sells many automatic blood pressure monitors, and the model numbers are easy to mix up. This guide compares common Omron home monitors by their HEM model numbers, using Omron's own Asia-Pacific product pages and instruction manuals. It explains what each feature does and what to check before you order. It does not tell you what your readings mean. That is for your doctor.

How are Omron BP monitor models named?

Every Omron monitor has a HEM model number on the back label, and that number is the most reliable way to compare models. Omron's Asia-Pacific pages also group monitors into tiers: Standard models such as the HEM-7120, HEM-7121, HEM-8712 and wrist HEM-6161, Plus models such as the HEM-7156 and HEM-7155T, and Elite models such as the HEM-7361T.

The same monitor can carry a different name in another country, and Omron's country sites list different ranges. In the models we checked, a T at the end of the number marked a Bluetooth version, for example HEM-7156 and HEM-7156T. Always compare the full HEM number, including any letters after it.

What do the common Omron features mean?

Most Omron home monitors share a set of named features, and the product pages explain each one:

  • IntelliSense: the monitor inflates the cuff to a suitable level each time, with no setting for the user to choose.
  • Cuff wrapping guide: shows OK when the cuff is wrapped correctly.
  • Body movement detection: prompts you to repeat the reading if you moved.
  • Hypertension indicator: the heartbeat symbol blinks if a reading is above 135 systolic or 85 diastolic mmHg, the standard range Omron uses.
  • Irregular heartbeat detection: a symbol appears if the rhythm varies by more than 25% from the average, more than twice during a reading. Omron says to repeat the reading and consult a health professional if it keeps appearing.
  • Averaging: Plus and Elite models show an average of the last three readings taken within 10 minutes.

Should you choose an upper arm or wrist Omron monitor?

Most of Omron's range is upper arm, and only one wrist model appears in this comparison. The HEM-6161 wrist monitor fits wrists from 13.5 to 21.5 cm, weighs about 85 g without batteries and runs about 300 readings on two AAA batteries, which suits travel. Upper arm models are larger and some accept an optional mains adapter.

If you are unsure, ask your doctor which type they want you to use. Our guide to upper arm vs wrist BP apparatus covers this in more depth, and how to check blood pressure at home covers technique.

Which Omron cuff size do you need?

Measure around the middle of the upper arm with a tape and match it to the cuff range. The HEM-7120 manual gives its supplied cuff as 22 to 32 cm. The IntelliWrap cuff on the HEM-7155T is listed as 22 to 42 cm. Omron also lists optional cuffs for several models: a small cuff for 17 to 22 cm, a large cuff for 32 to 42 cm and a wide range cuff for 22 to 42 cm.

A cuff outside your arm range is a common cause of wrong readings, and the HEM-6161 manual says to use the monitor only on people whose wrist is within the cuff range. If more than one person will use the monitor, check every user's arm.

What does the AFib indicator on the HEM-7361T do?

The HEM-7361T measures blood pressure and checks for signs of atrial fibrillation (AFib) in the same reading, according to Omron. It stores 100 readings for each of two users and sends them to the OMRON Connect app over Bluetooth.

An AFib symbol is a prompt to speak to a doctor, not a diagnosis. Only a doctor can decide what it means. If a family member has a heart rhythm condition, ask their doctor whether this feature is useful for them.

Which Omron monitors are validated in pregnancy?

Omron's product pages state that the HEM-7156, HEM-7156T and HEM-7361T are validated for accuracy during pregnancy, and that the HEM-7155T is validated during pregnancy with and without pre-eclampsia. The HEM-6161 manual says that wrist model has not been validated for use on pregnant patients.

Blood pressure in pregnancy needs medical supervision. Follow your doctor's advice on which device to use and how often.

How accurate are Omron monitors?

Omron's pages mark these models as clinically validated, and the HEM-6161 manual gives the detail: pressure accuracy of plus or minus 3 mmHg, pulse within 5% of the display, and clinical testing to ISO 81060-2:2013. The manual also lists conditions that can affect any reading, including arrhythmias, poor circulation, diabetes, pregnancy, kidney disease and movement or shivering.

For more on what validation means, see BP monitor validation explained. The HEM-6161 manual gives a service life of 5 years for that model, so ask how old a monitor is before you rely on it.

What should you check before buying an Omron monitor in Pakistan?

  1. Full HEM number on the box and the back of the monitor, including any T or other letters.
  2. Cuff range against the arm size of every user.
  3. Memory and users: one user with last reading, or two users with dated memory.
  4. Phone app: for T models, check Omron's list of supported phones before you rely on OMRON Connect.
  5. Power: battery type, and whether a mains adapter is included or optional.
  6. English manual and written warranty in the box.

Frequently asked questions

What is the difference between Omron HEM-7120 and HEM-7121?

Both are Standard upper arm monitors with irregular heartbeat detection. The HEM-7120 keeps only the last reading, while the HEM-7121 stores 30 readings.

What does the T mean in Omron HEM-7156T?

In the models we checked, the T version adds Bluetooth so readings can be sent to the OMRON Connect app. The HEM-7156T also adds a guest mode.

Which Omron monitor detects AFib?

Among the models in this guide, the HEM-7361T has an AFib indicator. A symbol is a prompt to see a doctor, not a diagnosis.

What cuff size comes with the Omron HEM-7120?

The HEM-7120 manual lists a 22 to 32 cm arm cuff. Omron also lists a small 17 to 22 cm cuff as an option.

Is the Omron wrist monitor HEM-6161 suitable in pregnancy?

Its manual says it has not been validated for use on pregnant patients. Ask your doctor which device to use.

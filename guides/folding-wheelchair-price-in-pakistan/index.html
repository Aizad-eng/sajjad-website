Folding wheelchair price in Pakistan: steel vs aluminium, sizes

Updated November 1, 2026 · 7 min read

Quick answer

A folding manual wheelchair is the most common first wheelchair for home and hospital use. Two things drive most of the difference between models: the frame material, usually steel or aluminium, and the size and type of chair. This guide explains both, using the US Medicare wheelchair categories and a published manufacturer brochure, so you can compare quotes on like-for-like terms. It does not list prices, because they change with the model and the exchange rate.

What sets the price of a folding wheelchair in Pakistan?

The price of a folding wheelchair depends mostly on the frame material, the chair's weight, its size and weight capacity, the brand and the import cost on the day you order.

  • Frame material: steel or aluminium, with titanium and carbon at the top end.
  • Weight class: lightweight and ultralightweight chairs are separate categories from standard chairs, so the quote should say which one you are getting.
  • Size and capacity: wider seats and heavy-duty frames for larger users change the model and the price.
  • Type: self-propelled chairs with large rear wheels, or transit chairs pushed by a carer.
  • Features: removable or flip-back armrests, swing-away footrests, anti-tippers and a quick-release rear wheel.
  • Brand, exchange rate and freight: imported chairs move with the rupee rate, and duties change, so check the current rule.

What is the difference between a steel and an aluminium wheelchair?

The main difference is weight: an aluminium folding chair is usually lighter than a steel one of the same design, and it costs more. Wikipedia's overview of wheelchair design describes the low-cost end of the market as heavy folding steel chairs with sling seats, with the same folding design made in aluminium at a slightly higher price band. It also notes that attendant-pushed chairs are usually built from steel, because weight matters less when the user does not push the chair.

A steel frame suits a chair that stays mostly in one place, such as a hospital ward or a ground-floor home. An aluminium frame suits a user who lifts the chair into a car often or pushes it a lot. Material alone does not decide quality. The US Medicare policy article on manual wheelchair bases says its categories include construction of any material, including titanium and carbon, and sorts chairs by weight and features instead. Ask for the actual weight of the model you are quoted.

How much does a folding wheelchair weigh?

A standard folding wheelchair weighs more than about 16 kg, while lightweight and ultralightweight chairs come in under that figure. The US Centers for Medicare and Medicaid Services (CMS) groups manual wheelchairs as shown below. Weight is the chair itself without the front rigging, meaning the footrests or legrests.

These are US billing categories, not Pakistani rules, but they give you a shared language. If a seller calls a chair "lightweight", ask for the weight in kilograms and whether it includes the footrests.

What seat sizes do folding wheelchairs come in?

Adult folding wheelchairs are sold in several seat widths, so you order the width that fits the user. The Invacare Action 2 NG brochure (UK, 2016) is a good example: it lists seat widths of 380, 405, 430, 455 and 480 mm, seat depths of 400 or 430 mm, and a maximum user weight of 125 kg.

The right width depends on the user's body and needs, so choose it at a fitting. The World Health Organization (WHO) Wheelchair provision guidelines (2023) say the best outcomes come from individual assessment, fitting, training and follow-up by trained personnel. Take measurements with the person seated and ask a trained person for advice. Our guide on how to choose a wheelchair walks through fitting step by step.

Will a folding wheelchair fit through your doors?

Check the overall open width of the chair, not the seat width, because the wheels and push rims add to it. Invacare gives the overall width of the Action 2 NG as the seat width plus 200 mm for the self-propelled version and plus 145 mm for the transit version. A 455 mm seat on the self-propelled model is therefore about 655 mm wide when open.

Measure your narrowest door, often the bathroom, and leave room for hands on the push rims. Also check the folded size against your car boot.

Self-propelled or transit wheelchair: which do you need?

Choose a self-propelled chair if the user will push themselves, and a transit chair if a carer will always push. Self-propelled chairs have large rear wheels with push rims. Transit chairs have small rear wheels, so they are narrower and lighter. On the Action 2 NG, the brochure gives weights of 14.9 kg and 16.2 kg across its versions, and states a weight from 14.9 kg. Transit chairs are useful for hospital transfers, short trips and tight homes.

What weight capacity do you need?

Pick a chair whose rated user weight is clearly above the user's weight. In the CMS categories, standard and lightweight chairs are rated for users up to about 113 kg, heavy-duty chairs for heavier users and extra heavy-duty chairs for users over about 136 kg. The Action 2 NG is rated at 125 kg. Wider seats and heavy-duty frames can add weight, so ask for the chair weight and the capacity together.

Can a folding wheelchair be used as a seat in a vehicle?

Only if the maker has crash-tested it for that use, and even then a car seat is safer. The Action 2 NG brochure states the chair is crash tested to ISO 7176-19 and also lists ISO 10542, but Invacare advises users to transfer to the vehicle seat and use the vehicle's own restraint system whenever it is feasible. If a chair's brochure makes no crash-test claim, do not use it as a seat in a moving vehicle.

What should you check before ordering?

  1. Frame material and the chair's weight in kilograms, with and without footrests.
  2. Seat width, seat depth and seat height, and the overall open and folded width.
  3. Maximum user weight.
  4. Self-propelled or transit, and the type of tyres (solid or air-filled).
  5. Whether armrests and footrests flip back or swing away for transfers.
  6. Brakes, anti-tippers and the availability of spare tyres and castors.
  7. Warranty terms in writing.

Frequently asked questions

Which is better, a steel or an aluminium wheelchair?

Aluminium chairs are lighter and easier to lift into a car, while steel chairs are heavier and sit at the lower-cost end. For a chair that stays at home or on a ward, steel is often enough.

How much does a standard folding wheelchair weigh?

Under the US CMS categories a standard chair weighs more than 36 lb, about 16.3 kg, without footrests. Lightweight chairs weigh about 15.4 to 16.3 kg and ultralightweight chairs under about 13.6 kg.

What seat width should I order?

The Invacare Action 2 NG, for example, comes in seat widths from 38 to 48 cm. Choose the width at a fitting with the user seated, as the WHO wheelchair guidelines recommend individual assessment.

What is a transit wheelchair?

A transit wheelchair has small rear wheels and is pushed by a carer. It is narrower and lighter than a self-propelled chair of the same range.

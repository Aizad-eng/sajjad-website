Physiotherapy clinic equipment list: electrotherapy, exercise and assessment tools

Updated November 1, 2026 · 8 min read

Quick answer

A physiotherapy clinic equipment list usually covers treatment plinths, electrotherapy (TENS, interferential, muscle stimulation), therapeutic ultrasound, hot and cold packs, traction, exercise items such as weights, bands, balls and parallel bars, and assessment tools such as a goniometer and BP apparatus. Requirements vary by country and province, so check with your regulator.

A physiotherapy clinic can start small, with a couch, a few electrotherapy units and basic exercise equipment, and grow from there. The hard part is deciding what to buy first. There is no single international list, so this guide uses one detailed published example: the National Standards for Physiotherapy Services from the Ministry of Health of Bhutan, which includes a full equipment annexure. It also covers the IEC safety standards for electrotherapy and ultrasound units. Requirements in Pakistan vary by province, so use this as a planning checklist.

What equipment does a physiotherapy clinic need?

A physiotherapy clinic needs treatment couches, electrotherapy and heat or cold equipment, exercise and gait equipment, and simple assessment tools. Bhutan's national physiotherapy standards (first published 2011) group equipment into categories that include electrotherapeutic modalities, diathermy, cold therapy, heat therapy, ultrasound, traction, respiratory therapy, weights and resistance, exercise therapy, postural support, mobility and gait training, splinting, braces and supports, and instruments. A small outpatient clinic usually needs only some of these, chosen to match its services.

Which items should a small clinic buy first?

A small clinic should buy for the services it will offer on day one and add specialised units later. The Bhutan list itself marks basic items, such as treatment plinths, TENS units, hot packs, exercise balls, parallel bars and assessment tools, for more facility levels than specialised items such as therapeutic pools, ultraviolet equipment or a multijoint isokinetic system. A practical first order for an outpatient clinic is:

  1. Treatment plinths or couches, one per treatment bay, plus bolsters and wedges.
  2. Electrotherapy: TENS, interferential or muscle stimulation units.
  3. Therapeutic ultrasound.
  4. Hot and cold packs with a heating unit.
  5. Exercise basics: weights, resistance bands, balls, mats, a mirror and a static bicycle.
  6. Assessment tools: goniometer, measuring tape, reflex hammer and a BP apparatus.

Which treatments suit which patient is a clinical decision for the physiotherapist. This list is about equipment only.

Which consumables and supports does a physiotherapy clinic reorder?

A physiotherapy clinic reorders electrode and gel supplies most often, along with packs, tapes and supports. The Bhutan list names consumables next to each machine and has separate sections for splinting materials and for braces and supports. Items from those sections include:

  • Electrotherapy and ultrasound: electrodes, gels and spray, and ultrasound gel.
  • Heat and cold: hot pack covers, heat pads, and reusable and disposable ice packs.
  • Splinting: heat-mouldable thermoplastic sheets, hook and loop strips, stockinet, and plaster of Paris or cast materials.
  • Braces and supports: soft and hard cervical collars, lumbosacral belts, crepe bandages, ankle braces, arm slings, elastic knee braces and knee immobilisers.
  • Tape: athletic tape.

Keep a simple stock sheet for these items so you reorder before you run out. Supports come in sizes, so record the size split you use most.

What safety standards apply to electrotherapy and ultrasound units?

Electrotherapy and therapeutic ultrasound units fall under particular standards in the IEC 60601 series for medical electrical equipment. Two are most relevant:

  • IEC 60601-2-10:2012 covers the basic safety and essential performance of nerve and muscle stimulators used in physical medicine, and names TENS and EMS devices. Amendments were published in 2016 and 2023.
  • IEC 60601-2-5:2009 covers ultrasonic physiotherapy equipment with one unfocused circular transducer per treatment head.

When you compare models, ask the supplier which edition of each standard the unit was tested to and for a copy of the test report summary. Editions change, so check the current one at the time of writing (November 2026).

What does published guidance say about looking after the equipment?

Published guidance expects equipment to be safe, maintained on a routine schedule and used only by trained staff. Bhutan's standards say equipment should meet applicable health, safety and accessibility requirements, be maintained routinely, and that specialised equipment should only be operated by appropriately qualified staff approved by the facility. They also ask for biomedical engineering support for installation of sophisticated equipment and for breakdowns. For a private clinic, that means asking who will install each unit, who will service it, and how long spare parts such as electrodes, cables and ultrasound heads will be available.

What emergency equipment should a physiotherapy clinic have?

A physiotherapy clinic should be ready to start resuscitation, because Bhutan's standards call for provision for emergency resuscitation in life-threatening situations. A good reference for the kit is the Resuscitation Council UK minimum list for primary care: gloves, aprons and eye protection, an adult pocket mask with an oxygen port, an oxygen cylinder and tubing, an automated external defibrillator (AED) with adhesive pads and a spare set, a razor, a stethoscope and an absorbent towel. Train staff on whatever you buy.

Which Pakistani rules apply to a physiotherapy clinic?

Ask your provincial healthcare regulator, because published clinic standards differ by province. At the time of writing (November 2026), the Punjab Healthcare Commission's standards download page lists sets for hospitals, GP and specialist clinics, dental clinics, clinical laboratories and other facility types, but no separate physiotherapy set. Check with the Commission or your own provincial regulator which category your clinic falls under.

How should you plan a physiotherapy equipment order?

Plan the order around your treatment bays, your services and the consumables each unit uses. Count the bays, list the services, then match equipment to each service. Add consumables such as electrodes, gel, hot pack covers and resistance bands, which you will reorder often. Frequently asked questions

What is the basic equipment for a physiotherapy clinic?

Published lists such as Bhutan's include treatment plinths, a TENS or interferential unit, therapeutic ultrasound, hot and cold packs, exercise equipment and assessment tools such as a goniometer, measuring tape and reflex hammer.

Which standard covers TENS and muscle stimulators?

IEC 60601-2-10:2012, with amendments in 2016 and 2023, covers the basic safety and essential performance of nerve and muscle stimulators, including TENS and EMS devices.

Which standard covers physiotherapy ultrasound machines?

IEC 60601-2-5:2009 covers ultrasonic physiotherapy equipment. Ask the supplier which edition a model was tested to.

Does Punjab have separate standards for physiotherapy clinics?

At the time of writing, the Punjab Healthcare Commission's standards download page does not list a separate physiotherapy set. Ask the Commission which category applies to your clinic.

Does a physiotherapy clinic need emergency equipment?

Bhutan's national standards call for provision for emergency resuscitation. The Resuscitation Council UK primary care list, with a pocket mask, oxygen and an AED, is a useful reference.

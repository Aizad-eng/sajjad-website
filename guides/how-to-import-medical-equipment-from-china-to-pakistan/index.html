How to import medical equipment from China to Pakistan: routes, DRAP and Alibaba

Updated November 1, 2026 · 9 min read

Quick answer

You can buy Chinese medical equipment from a local DRAP licensed distributor, import it yourself, or order through a sourcing service. Commercial imports need a DRAP establishment licence, product enlistment or registration and a DRAP clearance certificate at customs. On Alibaba, choose a Verified Supplier, pay through Trade Assurance and ask for CE, ISO 13485 and, where relevant, FDA documents.

Many clinics and businesses in Pakistan look at buying medical equipment directly from Chinese suppliers. This guide sets out the three practical routes, the DRAP and customs points you need to plan for, how to judge a Chinese supplier on Alibaba, which certificates to ask for, and how air shipping works. It reflects the rules and pages we checked at the time of writing (November 2026). It does not quote duties, taxes or fees, because these change and must be checked on official sources at the time of import.

What are the ways to get medical equipment from China into Pakistan?

There are three common routes. Which one suits you depends on the device, the quantity and whether you plan to resell.

The first route puts the regulatory work on a business that is already licensed. The second gives you the most control but carries the full set of DRAP duties. The third can help with one-off items, such as a single replacement part, but the regulatory questions still apply.

What does DRAP require before medical devices are imported?

Under Rule 21 of the DRAP Medical Devices Rules, 2017, a medical device may be imported only if the importer holds a valid establishment licence, the device is enlisted (Class A) or registered (Classes B, C and D), and the importer has premises for proper storage. Enlistment or registration can only be applied for by a licensed manufacturer or a licensed importer acting as the authorised representative of the foreign maker, so a Chinese factory cannot register a product in Pakistan by itself.

The Rules also have narrow exceptions: import permits for small quantities for testing or clinical investigation, personal use imports, and hospital imports of unregistered devices for their own patients with prior approval. Each has conditions. Our guide to the DRAP Medical Devices Rules explains them in more detail.

What happens at customs in Pakistan?

Rule 26 of the Medical Devices Rules says no medical device, component or raw material may be released from customs until the importer has a clearance certificate from an officer authorised by DRAP. If customs or DRAP suspect a device does not comply, the matter can be referred to DRAP's Director of Medical Devices.

Import declarations are filed through Pakistan Single Window (PSW), which describes itself as a single platform for import, export and transit documents. PSW lists DRAP among the government agencies integrated into the system for licences, permits and certificates.

Duties and taxes depend on the product's tariff code. The Federal Board of Revenue publishes the Pakistan Customs Tariff on its website, with editions up to 2026-27 listed when we checked. We do not state rates in this guide. Check the current tariff for your exact product, and use a licensed customs agent for the declaration.

How do you choose a Chinese supplier on Alibaba?

Alibaba.com lists many medical equipment sellers, from factories to trading companies. A few checks help you narrow the field.

  • Verified Supplier status. Alibaba says Verified Suppliers have had on-site verification by a third-party inspection company, which produces an assessment report that buyers can view. It checks the company, not each order.
  • Manufacturer or trader. Ask whether the seller makes the product or resells it. If it resells, ask for the name of the actual manufacturer, because that is the name that should appear on certificates.
  • Exact model and datasheet. Ask for the model number, the full datasheet and the instructions for use in English. Compare them with the maker's own documents where possible.
  • Samples. For larger orders, order one unit first and check it before committing to a bigger batch.

We have sourced a TDH39/DD45-type audiometric headset as a single piece from a verified Alibaba supplier and delivered it in Pakistan, so single unit orders are possible on the platform.

How does Alibaba Trade Assurance protect your payment?

Trade Assurance is Alibaba's buyer protection programme. Alibaba says it applies to orders agreed with a supplier on Alibaba.com and paid through the platform, by online checkout or by a wire transfer made via Alibaba.com. Payment is held until you receive and confirm the product, and you can request a refund if an order never ships, goes missing, or arrives defective, incorrect or damaged.

When Alibaba launched the upgraded programme in 2015, it said each qualified supplier has a Trade Assurance amount that caps the cover available, and that buyers can choose quality protection before shipment or after delivery. Read the current terms on your order before you pay, and do not agree to pay outside the platform if you want this protection.

Which certificates should you ask a Chinese supplier for?

  • CE marking documents. In the EU, notified bodies carry out conformity assessment where a third party assessment is required, and the European Commission lists them in its NANDO database. Ask for the EU certificate and declaration of conformity, and check the notified body name and number in NANDO. Interacoustics, for example, names TÜV, identification number 0123, in its AD629 specification.
  • ISO 13485. This is the international standard for medical device quality management systems. The US FDA incorporated ISO 13485:2016 by reference into its Quality Management System Regulation, effective 2 February 2026. Ask for the certificate and check that the company name, address and scope match the product.
  • FDA, where relevant. Be careful with "FDA certificates". The FDA says it does not issue registration certificates to device facilities, and that a registration and listing entry does not mean approval or clearance. If a supplier claims FDA clearance, ask for the 510(k) or approval number and check it in the FDA's Devices@FDA database.
  • Free sale evidence. DRAP's rules refer to free sale in the country of origin for some import routes, so ask whether the supplier can provide it.

Keep in mind that these certificates help a licensed importer with DRAP enlistment or registration. They do not replace it.

How is medical equipment shipped from China to Pakistan by air?

Small items such as audiometer parts, monitors and instruments can travel by air, either as air cargo through a freight forwarder or by express courier. Ask the supplier to quote the shipping method, packed weight and dimensions, and the incoterm, so you know who pays for which part of the journey.

Devices with lithium batteries need extra care. IATA's rules for batteries contained in or packed with equipment depend on the battery's watt-hour rating or lithium content, and an extra packaging requirement for these shipments took effect in January 2025. Ask the supplier to pack and declare batteries to IATA rules, and give your forwarder the battery details before booking.

Remember that the DRAP clearance certificate is needed before customs releases the goods, so arrange the paperwork before the shipment lands.

How can you reduce the risk on a first order?

  1. Decide the route first: local distributor, own import or sourcing service.
  2. Confirm the DRAP status of the exact product and who will hold the licence and registration.
  3. Choose a Verified Supplier, ask for datasheets and certificates, and check them.
  4. Pay through Trade Assurance and read the order terms.
  5. Agree packing, battery handling and shipping method in writing.
  6. Plan installation, calibration and service in Pakistan before the item arrives, if the device needs them.

Frequently asked questions

Can I import medical equipment from China without a DRAP licence?

For commercial import, Rule 21 of the Medical Devices Rules requires an establishment licence and product enlistment or registration. Narrow exceptions exist for personal use, testing and hospital patients, each with conditions.

What is an Alibaba Verified Supplier?

Alibaba says it is a supplier that has had on-site verification by a third-party inspection company, with an assessment report buyers can view. It checks the company, not each order.

Does Trade Assurance cover payments made outside Alibaba?

Alibaba says Trade Assurance applies to orders agreed on Alibaba.com and paid through the platform. Paying outside it means relying on the supplier alone.

Is an FDA registration certificate proof of approval?

No. The FDA says it does not issue registration certificates and that registration does not mean approval or clearance. Check clearance in Devices@FDA.

What customs duty applies to medical equipment in Pakistan?

It depends on the tariff code of the product. Check the current Pakistan Customs Tariff published by FBR and ask a licensed customs agent.

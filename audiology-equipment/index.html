Audiometers

Screening and diagnostic audiometers for clinics, ENT practices and hearing centres.

Questions

Can you find a replacement headphone for my audiometer?

Usually. Send us your audiometer's make and model and the headphone type it uses, such as TDH39 or DD45, and we will source a compatible one.

Will a new headphone need calibration?

Audiometer transducers are calibrated together with the audiometer, so plan for calibration after a replacement. We source the equipment but do not provide calibration.

Do you sell single units?

Yes. One of our orders was a single audiometric headset, sourced from a verified supplier and delivered in Pakistan.

Can you source a complete audiometer?

Yes. Tell us whether you need a screening or diagnostic audiometer and the tests you run, and we will come back with options and a price in rupees.
